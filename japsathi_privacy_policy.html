Privacy Policy

JapSathi — Naam Jap Counter & Daily Mantra

Effective Date: October 25, 2026

App Name: JapSathi — Naam Jap Counter & Daily Mantra

1. Introduction

JapSathi (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) is a devotional mantra-counting application designed to help users practise jap, track their spiritual progress, manage mantras, and maintain daily chanting habits.

This Privacy Policy explains how JapSathi handles information when you use the application.

2. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use and the app configuration, JapSathi may handle the following information:

  • Jap and mantra data: Mantra names, mantra text, jap counts, mala sizes, personal targets, and lifetime totals.
  • Session and history data: Session start times, duration, counts, speed, optional notes, and historical statistics.
  • Preferences: Theme, accent colour, vibration and sound preferences, daily goals, reminder schedules, and other settings.
  • Backup data: Information included in a backup that you choose to export or restore.
  • Notification information: Reminder schedules and notification preferences required to provide reminder functionality.

Personal notes or custom mantra text may contain information you choose to enter. Please avoid including sensitive personal information in optional notes.

3. How Information Is Stored

JapSathi is designed to maintain your chanting records and preferences on your device, subject to the application's actual implementation.

If data is stored locally, it generally remains on your device unless you choose to export, share, or otherwise transfer it. Local data may be lost if you uninstall the application, clear its storage, or lose your device.

JapSathi's backup and restore feature allows you to export and restore supported app data. Exported backups may be accessible to other applications or services if you choose to share them.

4. Advertising and Third-Party Services

If the installed version of JapSathi includes Google AdMob or another advertising service, that service may collect or process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, app interactions, diagnostics, and information required to deliver or measure advertisements, according to its configuration and applicable policies.

Third-party services operate under their own privacy policies. We do not claim control over information independently collected by those services.

If the app does not include advertising or other third-party data-collection SDKs, this section should be revised to reflect that fact.

5. Notifications and Home-Screen Widgets

JapSathi may schedule local reminders based on the times and preferences you configure. Notifications may display your selected mantra or daily goal.

The home-screen widget may display your selected mantra, jap count, current bead, completed malas, and today's total.

These features are intended to provide quick access to app functionality. Their exact data handling depends on the operating system and app implementation.

6. Data Sharing

JapSathi does not intentionally sell your personal information.

Information may be processed or shared with third-party service providers when necessary to operate enabled services, such as advertising, diagnostics, or other integrations. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law.

The extent of any sharing depends on the SDKs, permissions, and services enabled in the published version of the app.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

You can use the app's available controls to reset jap counts, delete individual sessions, clear history, or erase app data.

Resetting a current count does not necessarily delete lifetime totals or saved history.

Exported backups and copies shared outside the app may remain in the locations where they were saved. You are responsible for managing those copies.

Data retained by third-party service providers, if any, is subject to their applicable retention and deletion policies.

8. Permissions

Depending on your device and installed version, JapSathi may request permissions or use operating-system capabilities for:

  • Notifications and scheduled reminders.
  • Haptic feedback and vibration.
  • Volume-button interactions.
  • Home-screen widget updates.
  • Keeping the screen active during jap.

The app should request only permissions and capabilities needed for its functionality.

9. Children's Privacy

JapSathi is a general devotional utility and is not specifically intended to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a service integrated into the app, contact us so we can review the matter.

Any applicable child-directed app requirements and advertising restrictions must be followed where relevant.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the app. However, no storage method or electronic transmission is completely secure. Please keep exported backups in a safe location and protect access to your device.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features, services, or applicable requirements change. The updated policy will be published at the same policy URL with a revised effective date when appropriate.
